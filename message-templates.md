# Message templates

The app fills the `{placeholders}` and sends these exact words. Edit the wording here; no code change needed.

## greeting
Namaste {store_name}! Aapka order mil gaya. Ek minute, check kar rahe hain...

## understood
Humne ye samjha:

## followup_choice
"{phrase}" — kaunsa chahiye? {options}

## followup_quantity
"{phrase}" — kitna bhejna hai? (jaise: 2 peti, 5 packet)

## followup_unit
"{phrase}" ke liye "{unit_word}" ka matlab samajh nahi aaya. Peti, dabba ya packet?

## not_in_catalog
Maaf kijiye, "{phrase}" hamare paas abhi nahi hai. Baaki order ready hai.

## nudge
Aapne is baar {product} order nahi kiya, bhej dein?

## nudge_declined
Theek hai, {product} nahi bhejenge.

## quantity_flag
Dhyaan dein: {product} ki quantity ({qty}) aapke usual order se kaafi zyada hai. Sahi hai to tick karein.

## injection_reply
Hum sirf order le sakte hain. Rate ya discount ke liye apne sales officer se baat karein.

## confirm_prompt
Kripya order aur total check karke "Confirm" dabayein. Confirm ke baad hi order dispatch hoga.

## confirmed
Dhanyavaad! Order {order_id} confirm ho gaya. Total: Rs {total}.
